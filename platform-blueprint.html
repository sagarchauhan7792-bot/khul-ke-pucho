Khul Ke Pucho · Platform blueprint

Build the brand as a platform, not a product catalogue.

Assessment → personalisation → kit → practice → follow-up. This document sets out the architecture we are building, how the offer is structured, how growth is intended to compound, and the safeguards that sit around all of it.

01 · Thesis

People buy random products without understanding the underlying pattern

Sexual concerns are individual and usually involve body, mind, lifestyle and partner context at the same time. A private bilingual assessment that routes to a personalised plan — products, practices and follow-up — is a fundamentally stronger business than a shelf of formulations, because it earns the right to the second and third purchase.

Problem

Undiagnosed buying

The same product is sold to people with completely different causes, so most of them do not work — and do not come back.

Insight

Pattern, not symptom

Timing, erection, desire and comfort concerns share overlapping drivers: sleep, metabolic health, stress and the relationship.

Solution

Assess → personalise → support

A structured, private, bilingual front door that produces a plan and then tracks whether it worked.

Verticals

Women as a core vertical

Men, women and couples as three connected journeys — with women built as an independent vertical rather than an add-on.

10 · Safeguards

Personalisation must never become automatic prescribing

Medical safety, privacy and clinician review are visible parts of the product, not disclaimers bolted on at the end. In this category, trust is destroyed once and never rebuilt.

Assessment

Consent-first

Collect only what is needed; explain why each question is asked.

Decision layer

Triage, not diagnosis

Rules identify when a case must go to clinician review rather than to a product.

Prescription

Compliant workflow only

Prescription medicines only through a proper clinical workflow — never through a quiz outcome.

Devices

Verified before listing

Classification, sourcing and compliance confirmed before anything is sold.

Claims

No cures, no shame

No guaranteed outcomes, no exaggerated performance claims, no fear-based selling.

Data

Security by default

Sensitive information requires strong access control and a transparent privacy policy.

Don'tAssume every woman has the same desire, or the same needs.
Don'tUse fear or shame to sell anything.
Don'tPromise guaranteed orgasm, performance or cures.
Don'tTreat every symptom as a product problem.
Don'tLet a quiz produce an automatic diagnosis.
DoBuild clear clinician-review and referral pathways, and make them visible.

13 · Research notes

Sources behind the market discussion

Market estimates vary materially by methodology, so the direction matters more than any single number. Use verified current figures and your own pilot data before presenting financial projections.

  • IMARC — India Sexual Wellness Market: overview, online distribution and category segmentation.
  • Research and Markets — India Sexual Wellness Market: category and forecast discussion.
  • Grand View Research — India Women's Health Market: growth and life-stage context.
  • Journal of Sex Research / PubMed literature — female sexual function domains and desire discrepancy.
  • Archives of Sexual Behavior / Springer — recent research on Indian women's sexual experiences.
  • AYUSH Suraksha — regulatory and advertising context for AYUSH medicines and claims.
The defensible market claim is greater awareness, autonomy and demand for support — not that behaviour itself has changed. Overstating that is both inaccurate and, in this category, reputationally expensive.
