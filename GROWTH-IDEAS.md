### ⚠️ Compliance note on #31 (important for ads & legal)
Never market the devices as **"medical equipment"** or promise outcomes ("will increase arousal time"):
- Medical-device claims invoke **CDSCO** device registration requirements.
- Outcome promises for sexual function risk the **Drugs & Magic Remedies Act**.
- Meta/Google reject adult products framed as treatments; education + "supports/comfort" framing passes review.
The live section uses population-framed statistics, "supports arousal and comfort" language, and an explicit "not a medical device" disclaimer — keep that framing in all future creative.
