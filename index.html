Aanand · The science of arousal

Her pleasure is physiology, not a mystery

Modern sexual-wellness practice treats arousal as something couples can understand and work with — openly, together.

15–20 min

Arousal takes time

Research consistently finds most women need 15–20 minutes of arousal before intercourse feels its best. Slowing down — massage, warmth, conversation — is not a detour; it is the route. Sparsh oil and the Jyoti candle are built for exactly this stage.

~70%

The right stimulation matters

Large surveys report a majority of women reach climax most reliably with external stimulation rather than intercourse alone. Body-safe devices like Tarang and Bindu exist to support this — which is why modern couples' counsellors discuss them without embarrassment.

pH 4.5

Comfort is a wellness issue

Dryness and discomfort are common and nothing to hide. A water-based, pH-balanced gel like Snigdha supports comfort and is condom-safe. If discomfort persists, that is a doctor conversation — and ours cost ₹99.

The questions everyone asks

Will anyone know what I ordered?
No. Every order — wellness or pleasure — ships in a plain brown parcel with no product name outside. The courier sees only a parcel; your invoice carries only the company's legal name.
Are the pleasure products body-safe?
Yes. All devices use medical-grade, body-safe silicone, are USB-rechargeable and splash-proof. Intimate care is water-based, pH-balanced and dermatologically tested. Materials and care instructions are on every product page.
Is the Ayurveda real, or bus-stand medicine?
Every formulation is AYUSH-licensed and every batch is lab-tested, with reports on request. We name every ingredient and cite its classical source. We never promise miracles — and we put that in writing.
Can I pay on delivery?
Yes. Cash on Delivery is available across India, along with UPI, cards and netbanking.
Can devices really help with arousal difficulties?
They can support arousal and comfort — that is why modern sexual-wellness practice discusses them openly. Most women need 15–20 minutes of arousal, and a majority respond best to external stimulation; body-safe devices and pH-balanced care products are tools for exactly that. They are personal-care items, not medicines: if a difficulty is persistent or painful, start with the ₹99 consult so a registered practitioner can look at the whole picture.
Can my partner and I shop or consult together?
Absolutely — that is the whole idea. The Aanand range is couples-first, and our doctors and counsellors see couples too. No judgment, ever.
How does the ₹99 consultation work?
Order it like any product. Your booking link arrives on WhatsApp and email — choose phone or video and a time that suits you. Your written plan follows within 24 hours.
