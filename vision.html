01 · The problem we are solving

Most people buy a product before anyone asks what is actually wrong

A friend recommends something. An ad promises everything. A chemist hands over a box. Nobody asks about sleep, stress, medication, the relationship, or how long this has been going on — and so the same product is sold to people with completely different problems. That is the model we are trying to replace.

04 · The distinction that changes everything

Desire ≠ arousal ≠ orgasm ≠ comfort

These are four different things, and they can move independently. Someone can have desire but little arousal; arousal but difficulty with orgasm; or both, with discomfort making intimacy hard. This is precisely why "one product for everyone" is a weak model.

Desire

"Do I want intimacy?"

The wish for sexual activity — shaped by stress, sleep, mood, medication and relationship context far more than most people expect.

Arousal

"Does my body respond?"

The physical and mental response — blood flow, lubrication, attention. It usually needs time: research points to roughly 15–20 minutes for many women.

Orgasm

"Can I reach climax?"

A separate question again. Large surveys find most women reach climax most reliably with external stimulation rather than intercourse alone — anatomy, not a verdict on anyone.

Comfort

"Is this physically comfortable?"

Dryness or pain is common and treatable — and the one domain where persistent symptoms should always be seen by a doctor rather than solved with a purchase.

Educational information for adults. Not medical advice, not a diagnosis, and not a promise of outcomes.

05 · On women's sexual wellness

Women are taking ownership of their sexual wellbeing

That is the shift worth building for — not a claim about how much sex anyone is having, but a real change in awareness, autonomy and willingness to ask for support. A woman's needs change through her life, and a good platform should follow her through it rather than sell her one box.

20s – 30sSelf-awareness, confidence, arousal, early relationships.
PostpartumComfort, body changes, recovery and returning to intimacy.
40s – 50sDesire changes, relationship shifts, hormonal questions.
Menopause+Comfort, desire, intimacy and general wellbeing.
